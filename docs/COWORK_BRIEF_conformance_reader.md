## Working rules

**Report, never repair** (0045 cl.2). The reader classifies. It does not add a
`Subject:` line to any ruling, does not edit a convention, and does not touch an
accepted entry's body under any circumstance (`CONVENTION_decisions.md`).

**The generated file is generated.** It is never hand-edited, and it carries the
`_`-prefix naming that `docs/_decisions_map.md` and `docs/_architecture_shape.md`
already use for authored/generated pairs (`CLAUDE.md`, **0030**). A code change
regenerates it in the same commit.

**Small and narrow beats large and rotting.** `auditor_doc_claims`' own docstring
is the standard this round builds to: *"it checks exactly ONE class of claim…
extend only to claims with one authoritative source."* If Task 3 wants to check
three things, it is three readers or it is one reader and two named gaps.

**A count published by this round carries its denominator** (0060 cl.3). This
applies to the round's own report, which is the first place the rule will be
tested.

3. **Build the reader, as one auditor with one claim class.** New file
   `auditors/auditor_rule_subjects.py`, registered in `verify.py`'s `AUDITORS`.

   recorded as subject-not-enumerable.** It reads `docs/DECISIONS.md` and

   Per 0060 clause 8 it must not fail on rules predating 0060; those classify as
   undeclared without going red, and the auditor states that carve-out in its own

4. **Generate the list.** `python run.py conformance-map` (name to match the

   its classification, and for declared-and-checked ones the reader that checks
   it. This is a **non-gating surface** (0031) — it reports; the auditor in Task
   3 is the only thing that can go red.

5. **Assert one round trip, and only one.** 0060 clause 5 in its cheapest honest
   form: a tester that takes each check which prints a remedy naming

   as of `154afbd`; Task 5 is to find whether any *other* check prints a remedy at

## Explicitly out of scope

- **Checking whether any rule is actually obeyed.** This round classifies
  subjects; it does not enforce a single ruling's content. The temptation is
  acute for 0056 gap 1 and 0055's undone registry migration, both of which are
  one small auditor away. Both are their own cards.
- **0051 clause 2's containment auditor.** The most serious gap in the estate by
  consequence, the same *shape* as this round, and **not this round**. Folding it
  in would put the estate's most sensitive material behind a reader built in its
  first week.
- **Retrofitting subjects onto 0001-0059.** 0060 clause 8 forbids it; the
  classification lives in the generated map, never in the entries.
- **Fixing the two-draft-directory hazard.** `data/decision_drafts/` and
  `data/decisions_draft/` both exist and both hold live drafts. Named in 0060's
  own header, tempting to tidy while appending 0060, and a separate act.
- **`CLAUDE.md`'s Debt at estate scale** — *"nothing checks any convention in
  this file"*. Task 3 will look like it could cover it. It cannot: `CLAUDE.md` is
  a map, its rules point elsewhere, and its environment hazards are prose by
  design (0052 cl.4).

## The one deliberate widening, named

**Task 2 writes a convention during a build round.** House practice is that a
convention is authored and a round applies it; here the convention does not exist
and 0060 clause 7 explicitly declines to discharge that debt by naming it. The
widening is bounded three ways: the convention may only carry what 0060 already
rules (it cites, it does not legislate); it is authored `not enforced` like every
other convention in `docs/`; and if writing it discovers a rule 0060 does not
cover, **that is a stop condition below, not a licence to add a clause.**

## Stop conditions

- **0060 is still `proposed` when code is about to be written** → stop. This is
  the precondition, and 0033 is the reason.
- **Task 2 discovers a rule the convention needs that 0060 does not contain** →
  stop. A convention inventing a rule is 0052 clause 3 inverted, and the remedy is
  a superseding entry, not a paragraph.
- **The auditor in Task 3 passes on its first run against the real tree** → stop.
  With 59 rulings and 11 conventions predating 0060, a reader that goes green
  immediately is measuring nothing. Suspect the carve-out in clause 8 has
  swallowed the subject.
- **The `[H]` count in the walk-sheet rises during the build** → stop. It means a
  property turned out awkward to check and the design is being adjusted to the
  test rather than the other way round.
- **You are about to make `_conformance_map.md` gate anything** → stop. It is a
  0031 surface; Task 3's auditor is the only verdict in this round.
- **The classification requires reading prose to decide** → stop, and record the
  rule as subject-not-enumerable. That is clause 2 working, and the instinct to
  "just read it and decide" is exactly the substitution that produced the 4-of-9
  figure.

## UAT — acceptance checks

Walk-sheet: `docs/UAT_conformance_reader.md`. Nothing is committed at walk time;
these check staged changes.

**Round falsifier, stated before the answer is known:** **does the generated list
name at least one rule the estate believed was enforced and is not?** If every
rule it flags is one somebody already knew about, the reader has produced a tidier
rendering of `AGENDA_design_gaps_2026-08-28.md` and bought bookkeeping — which is
0060's own third falsifier arriving early, and the consequence is that the 08-28
deferral was right and Cards C and D were the better week.

- **A1 [G].** 0060 appears in `docs/DECISIONS.md` with status `accepted <date>`,
  the date is later than its `Date:`, and `docs/_decisions_map.md` regenerated in
  the same commit reflects it.
- **A2 [G].** `docs/CONVENTION_conformance.md` exists, cites 0060 by clause, and
  introduces no rule absent from 0060 — checked by listing every imperative in
  the convention against 0060's clauses 1-8.
- **A3 [G].** `auditors/auditor_rule_subjects.py` is registered in `verify.py`'s
  `AUDITORS`, and `python verify.py` reports it by name.
- **A4 [G].** `python run.py conformance-map` writes `docs/_conformance_map.md`;
  running it twice with no intervening change produces a byte-identical file.
- **A5 [G].** Every rule in the map carries exactly one of the three
  classifications. None is blank, and the counts sum to the total.
- **A6 [G].** The map's own header states the denominator it counted over and how
  it was derived (0060 cl.3).
- **A7 [G].** The auditor's output states the 0060 clause 8 carve-out explicitly —
  a reader that silently excludes pre-0060 rules fails this check even if its
  numbers are right (0056 cl.1's second half).
- **A8 [G].** At least one rule classifies as `subject-not-enumerable`. If none
  does, clause 2's state is unreachable in practice and A8 is the check that says
  so. *(0057 clause 7 is the expected first member; the check does not require it
  to be that one.)*
- **A9 [G].** The brief's draft-status census (12 auditors / 82 testers) was
  re-derived at round-open and either confirmed or corrected in the report, with
  the correction recorded as an instance rather than a fix.
- **A10 [G].** Task 5 either lands a round-trip tester or the report records, with
  the search that established it, that no other check prints a remedy.
- **A11 [H].** Read `_conformance_map.md` cold. **Did it tell you something about
  this estate you did not already know**, or did you recognise every line? The
  round's falsifier, put to the operator in his own words.
- **A12 [H].** Having seen the list's length — would you rather not have it? An
  honest yes is a real finding about whether naming gaps drives closure or just
  produces a wall of red nobody reads (0048 cl.4 from the other direction).
- **A13 [H].** Did writing `CONVENTION_conformance.md` feel like *citing* 0060 or
  like *re-deciding* it? If the second, the widening above was not as bounded as
  this brief claims and the convention should be cut back before it lands.

**Three `[H]` checks, deliberately.** All three ask about the operator's
experience of the artefact rather than its behaviour, which is the only thing a
machine cannot get at here. Everything about the map's *structure* is `[G]`; only
its *worth* is `[H]`, and A12 is written to make "this was not worth building" an
easy answer to give.
