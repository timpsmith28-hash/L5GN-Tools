## Working rules

- **An artifact is a purchase with a claim attached.** Every distillation
  artifact (prompt, rubric, eval set) lands versioned, with a spend event,
  a target tier, and a *stated expected effect* ("K2 extraction acceptance
  at T2 rises"). No claim, no artifact — an unmeasurable purchase is
  D-B's laundering failure mode by construction.
- **The ledger judges the purchase, not the purchaser.** Acceptance-rate
  movement at the target tier, before/after, spread stated. No movement
  after a fair trial → that artifact shape stops being bought, and the
  report says so. The metric is retirement of future spend, measured —
  never the label on the purchase (INTENT §8's third failure mode).
- **The extraction ships a pattern, not the estate.** The package carries
  the manifest contract, the runner (lock, streaming, four outcomes), and
  the card/ruling shapes — and imports nothing from `l5gntools/` or
  `chronicler/`. The direction is enforced the way 0034 enforced the app
  tier: an auditor walks the imports and the gate fails a reach. A pattern
  extracted before the auditor exists is a promise, and promises decay
  (INTENT §5).
- **Extraction happens last because it must be earned.** A pattern that has
  not survived a quarter of personal use ships a guess to the one
  environment where you cannot iterate freely.

## Tasks

1. **The artifact registry** — versioned artifacts with spend events,
   target tiers, and expected effects; the before/after acceptance query;
   the quarterly answer to D-B's question (*what did frontier spend retire
   this quarter?*) rendered where the operator will actually see it.
2. **One real distillation walked end to end** — a T3 purchase (prepared
   handoff, per Phase 4) producing a rubric for a T2 stage; the trial run;
   the measured movement or its absence, reported either way.
3. **The extraction package** — named, minimal, dependency-direction
   audited; stood up against one real work repo's local scripts as its
   proving ground, with nothing personal in reach.
4. **The non-goal check** — re-read INTENT §4 against the package. If the
   extraction has grown product-shaped expectations (users who are owed,
   not consumers who complain), stop and re-argue the non-goal there, per
   its own instruction.

## Stop conditions

- An artifact lands without a spend event, target tier, or expected effect
  → stop.
- A purchase's claimed effect is asserted without the before/after query →
  stop.
- The package imports from `l5gntools/` or `chronicler/`, or the auditor
  doesn't exist before the package does → stop.
- Anything personal — vault data, personal-estate paths, the frontier tier
  — reaches the extracted package → stop (the wall, one repo further out).

## UAT — sketch (rewrite at round-open)

- `[G]` The dependency auditor fails a deliberately-planted reach from the
  package into the estate.
- `[G]` The acceptance query reproduces its numbers from ledger events
  alone, on a fresh checkout.
- `[H]` **The quarterly answer, read aloud**: did frontier spend retire
  future spend, with numbers? This is the vision's whole economic claim
  facing its audit.
- `[H]` **The work proving ground**: did the package run a real work repo's
  scripts under the manifest contract without modification beyond a
  manifest? Every gap is a finding about the pattern, found safely at home.
- `[H]` **The 2am test and INTENT §6, asked of Quartermaster entire** — one
  operator, alone, a year of accretion later: is this still a system you
  can hold? The honest answer closes the programme's arc either way.
